## How to add your event to the dashboard

These steps use the Wikimedia Outreach Dashboard, which tracks the edits made at your event.

1. Open the campaign dashboard.
2. **Log in** with your Wikimedia account.
   ![The Log in link on the dashboard](/assets/uploads/2024/03/Screenshot-2024-03-18-at-9.39.46-AM.png)
3. Select **Create Program +**.
   ![The Create Program button](/assets/uploads/2024/03/Screenshot-2024-03-18-at-9.50.55-AM.png)
4. In the pop-up window, select **Create New Program**.
5. Choose the type of program. If you're not sure, choose **Edit-a-Thon**.
6. Enter details about your program. Add your language, country, or location to the **Program title**, and include a contact email in the **Program Description** so people can reach you.
7. Set the dates and times to track. You may want to start a day or two before your event and end a day or two after.
8. Add any other information, then select **Create my Program!**

**Remember:** participants must join the program you create for their edits to be tracked.

For tips on running an event, see the [organizing materials](https://artandfeminism.org/resources/) from Art+Feminism.
