#### **This page includes resources for editing book-related content along with materials to support organizing events.**

##### **Logos and graphic assets**

Available on [Wikimedia Commons under the category EveryBookItsReader logos](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:EveryBookItsReader_logos) .

A new visual identity was developed for the campaign in 2024. We recommend that you use the 2024 version.

If you need a logo in another language, contact us by email (wikimediaeverybook@gmail.com ) and we will do our best to create it for you.
